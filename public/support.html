Common questions

  • My drive didn't record in the background. In iPhone Settings, set Smooth AF's Location to Always and turn on Precise Location.
  • The app can't find my OBD-II reader. Turn the car on, fully close the reader's own app, and keep Smooth AF open on the Garage screen. Readers must support Bluetooth LE.
  • Why does the score say "Settling"? The score hides for the first five minutes of moving, since it swings too much to mean anything that early.
  • How do I delete my account? Sign in, open the account screen and tap Delete account. See the Privacy Policy for what that removes.

Please drive safely

Set the app up before you drive, and don't touch your phone while moving. Always follow traffic laws and speed limits. Your score is never worth a risk.
