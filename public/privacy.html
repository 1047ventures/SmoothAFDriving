Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

Smooth AF grades how smoothly you drive and helps you get better. Doing that takes real data about your driving. This page says what we collect, who else sees it, and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • Location: your precise GPS route, speed and altitude while a drive is recording, including in the background if you allow it.
  • Motion: accelerometer and gyroscope readings during a drive, used to measure the ride. We save detailed readings only for a small number of accounts used to test scoring.
  • Drive results: scores, stops, harsh moments, points and timing.
  • Car data: if you connect an OBD-II reader, values such as throttle, RPM, speed and temperatures, plus your VIN and trouble codes.
  • Your garage: cars you add, which can include VIN, plate and insurance details if you enter them. These stay on your phone unless you have an account, in which case they sync to it, along with any social handles you add. Maintenance notes stay on your phone.
  • Destinations: places you search for or drive to.
  • Account: your name and email if you give them, and Apple's sign-in identifier if you use Sign in with Apple. You can use the app without an account.
  • Device: a random ID this app creates. It isn't tied to your phone's hardware or used for advertising.
  • Voice: if you start a concierge call, your voice during that call. We never use the microphone otherwise.

How we use it

To record and score your drives, show your history and progress, keep your drives across phones, look after your car, improve the scoring, and run the service. We don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or track you across other apps. We don't share it with insurers.

Who else sees it

We can read your data to run and support the service and to improve scoring. So can the services that run the app, for the purposes above:

  • Supabase and Cloudflare: store your account and drives, and host the app.
  • Mapbox, CARTO, OpenStreetMap services (Overpass, including the Kumi Systems mirror, and Nominatim) and OSRM: receive locations from your route or your searches to show maps, read traffic and speed limits, find places, and tell whether a stop was at a light.
  • Wikimedia Commons: receives car searches when you look for a picture of your car.
  • Google Fonts: supplies the app's fonts, and sees your IP address when it does.
  • NHTSA: receives your VIN or car model to identify the car and check recalls.
  • Vapi and its AI providers, including OpenAI: receive your voice and your car's readout (car details, trouble codes, battery, maintenance, recent drive scores) during a concierge call, and only after you agree. The readout doesn't include your locations.
  • Resend: sends sign-in emails and receives your email and name if you have an account.
  • Apple: if you use Sign in with Apple, or the Live Activity on your Lock Screen.
  • Spotify: only if you connect it.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting it

We keep your data until you delete it. To delete one drive, delete it in the app, which also removes the cloud copy. To delete everything, sign in and go to the account screen, then tap Delete account. That permanently removes your account, drives, garage and detailed sensor data from our systems, and clears the app's data on that phone.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access or correct your data, or object to how it's used. Use the contact on our support page.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Other drivers can't read your drives; only you and our operators can. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't promise absolute security.

Children

Smooth AF is for licensed drivers and isn't meant for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update this page and its date.
