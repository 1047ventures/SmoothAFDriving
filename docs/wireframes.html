User onboarding

A driver's first run

The path a brand-new driver takes from install to their third drive. Every step links to its wireframe below.

Ad splash Stale

Only for first visits that arrive from an ad link (utm_source). Either button closes it and reveals the intro.

Intro, 4 slides

"Every drive, scored" → edge glow → "Mount & drive" → privacy line. The last button asks for motion and location so the first drive starts clean.

Home, empty

Score "--", "First lap unscored. Tap Start Drive". Restore-from-cloud offer in the empty drive list.

First drive

Tap Start Drive. In iPhone Safari, a motion-permission card first; in the iOS app, nothing. Mount the phone and go.

End Drive → recap

Score, story of the drive, facts, harsh-moment map. Drives under 0.3 mi are discarded.

One post-drive ask

Signed out and first drive: "share & save" (share card + sign in). Otherwise, if we don't have a name, a name prompt. From drive 2 the "What are you driving?" card can stack on top of it.

Home learns

Improvement line says "N more drives and we'll show if you're getting smoother" until there are 6 comparable drives.

Plug in a reader

Car joins the Garage via VIN ("Is this your car?" on Home after the drive) and Car Health appears.

Navigation map

Where every tap goes

Screens are boxes, sheets and overlays are rounded. Dotted lines are automatic (no tap). Swipe right from the left edge goes back from any screen except Home and Drive.
