And you know it

You're the one who zippers the merge.

You're the one who's already in the right lane, watching ten cars ahead, while someone camps in the left lane doing 58. You're the one sitting behind a phone at a green light.

Driving well isn't a mystery. Read the road ahead. Know what's around you. See the whole flow of traffic instead of your own bumper. Most people were just never taught any of it, and it feels like it gets worse every damn year.

Those of us who know better have to do something about it.

And the training? In Michigan, turn 18 and you need no driver's ed at all to get a license.8 Plenty of states only require it if you're under 18. The people who study this call driver's ed "significantly under-funded and increasingly inaccessible."9

So we hand out licenses, never check in again, and act surprised by the numbers.

That's some bullshit. So we started building the fix.

The app

Every drive, scored.

Put your phone in the mount and drive. Smooth AF reads your phone's GPS and motion sensors and gives every drive a score out of 100, then tells you in plain words what to work on.

Whether you've been driving 20 years or 20 days.

One app that meets you where you are. A number to beat if you think you're already good, and a clear next step if you're just starting out.

  • A real score, every driveSmoothness, momentum and braking, out of 100, with the hard moments pinned on a map. New drivers see what to fix; car people get a number to beat.
  • Plain-words notes after every driveEach recap points to the moments that cost you, with one thing to try next time.
  • Calm cues while you driveThe screen edge glows green, amber or red. One short line at most, so your eyes stay on the road.
  • See yourself improveAfter a handful of drives, the app compares your last five with the ten before and tells you straight if you're getting better.
  • Plug into your carPair a Bluetooth OBD-II reader and the app reads throttle, RPM, gear and temperatures, and starts and ends drives on its own when the engine does.
  • Car healthCheck-engine codes in plain English, a battery check and open recalls for your car. A new driver learns what the light means; a car person gets the codes and readings.
  • LeaderboardPick a driver name and see where you rank.
  • Points for smooth miles10 points per smooth mile, more in heavy traffic, and badges from 10 to 1,000 miles.
  • Coaching that remembers you ComingIt tracks your habits across drives and backs off once you've fixed one.
  • Modes for first-timers and teens ComingCoaching tuned to where you are as a driver.

Scoring, points, progress and a basic car-health scan are free. No account needed to start; sign in later to back up your drives.

Where this goes

The driving school America never had.

Today's driving schools teach you to pass a test. We want to teach real skill: car control, reading the road, driving smooth because it's safer and it feels better. The app is step one. Here's the road from there.

  1. Live now

    The app

    Every drive scored and mapped, from GPS, motion sensors and the car itself.

  2. Live, growing

    Coaching in the app

    Plain-words notes on every drive today. Next it learns your habits and coaches you on the ones that stick.

  3. Building

    Human coaches

    Real coaches who review your actual drives, starting with teens and their parents.

  4. Building toward

    A national driving school

    Training built on what millions of real drives show, held to a higher bar than a "safe driver" checkbox.

Straight talk: driving schools are licensed state by state. We're not one yet. We're building toward it, one state at a time, the right way.
