Windshield cards

Four ways to say it, each 3.5 × 2 inches, a standard business card. Every card's code opens the free install page: no App Store, no account.

Printing at home: the PDF puts 10 cards on a letter sheet with cut marks. Use heavy matte card stock and the printer's best setting. Dark cards use a lot of ink, and edges can show a sliver of white if the cut is off.
Print shop: the per-card PDFs include the extra edge (bleed) shops ask for, so the dark runs cleanly to the cut.
