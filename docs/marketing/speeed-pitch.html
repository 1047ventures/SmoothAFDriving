What we are today

Early, real, and built.

  • A score out of 100 for every drive. Smoothness, momentum and braking, from the phone's GPS and motion sensors, with the hard moments pinned on a map.
  • Plug into the car. A Bluetooth OBD-II reader adds throttle, RPM, gear and plain-English check-engine codes.
  • Leaderboard, points and badges for smooth miles.
  • Free, and it runs in any phone's browser today. The iPhone app is in beta and heading to the App Store.
  • Coaching that remembers your habits Coming

Straight up: we're tiny. A solo founder, an invite-only beta, a handful of drivers and a couple hundred scored drives so far. That's exactly why you'd get a real say in what it becomes.
