The product

What Smooth AF is

What it does
Scores how smoothly you drive and coaches you to drive better. It uses the phone's motion sensors and GPS, plus the car's own computer when an OBD-II plug-in reader is connected.
Mission
Make people better drivers: efficient, smooth, reading the road ahead instead of reacting to it.
North Star
Improvement over time. The score, points and leaderboard keep people driving with the app; getting measurably better is the goal.
Platforms
Native iOS app via TestFlight, and a web app. No Android app.
Stage
Invite-only. At least three drivers besides the founder. No revenue.
Team
One founder, building with AI engineering sessions. 379 tasks shipped since April 30, 2026.
How we decide

Priority order

When two goals conflict, the higher one wins.

1st

Make a better driver

Features that change how someone drives ship before features that are only interesting.

2nd

Tell the truth

The app shows a blank rather than a guess, and "couldn't read it" rather than a false all-clear.

3rd

Safety third

Safety is the result of smooth driving, not the pitch. We don't market as a safety or insurance product. While the car moves, the app shows only glanceable feedback; decisions wait until you park.

Test for any feature: does this make someone a better driver?

Improvement verdict

Compares the last 5 drives with the 10 before: hard brakes, launches and turns per 10 miles (pooled by distance), share of smooth miles, pedal smoothness, and score. A change counts at 20% on a rate or 3 points on a score. Improvements are shown first; a slip is shown only if nothing improved.

Driving fingerprint

Where the scoring is heading

Two drivers can score the same through a corner. The accelerator pedal traced against sideways g shows the difference. The pedal is scored today; sideways g is not yet.

Brake and gun

Lift, brake hard into the apex, slam the throttle out. Late, lopsided g spike.

Carry the line

Ease off, carry speed to the apex, roll the throttle on. One even g curve.

Incidents that shaped the principles

Phantom turns
GPS heading jitter created false sharp turns; sideways g was removed from the score.
Silent leaderboard
Score uploads went to a table that didn't exist, hidden by an error handler, for months. Now built on the server from drives.
Open GPS tracks
A database rule let anyone with the public key read routes. Locked to owners and verified externally.
Code length
Sign-in sent an 8-digit code to a 6-character field. Found only by checking the live config.
Frozen deploy
A package added to one lockfile but not the other stopped web deploys for a day. Both lockfiles now change together.
Town scores
Pedal thresholds rated normal city driving 59–75 while highways got 95. Thresholds refitted to real drives.
Notes by role

What each role needs

Drivers

Using Smooth AF

What you get

  • A score out of 100 for every drive, with a map of harsh moments and why they happened.
  • 10 points per smooth mile, plus a bonus for beating the ETA while driving smoothly.
  • A line on Home telling you whether you're improving, and at what.
  • With an OBD-II plug-in reader: trouble codes in plain English, and your car added to the Garage automatically.

How we treat you

  • Your drives stay on your phone; the cloud copy is readable only by you.
  • Unavoidable stops at lights and stop signs barely count against you.
  • Anything that needs a decision waits until you've parked.
  • If something can't be measured properly, the app says so instead of guessing.
  • Points can't be redeemed yet.
CTO

Engineering

Architecture

  • Sensors: native Core Motion (custom Swift plugin, 60 Hz), native background GPS, ELM327 OBD reader over Bluetooth LE. Live Activity plugin for the Dynamic Island.
  • Scoring: pure JS on the phone (services/scoring.js), unit-tested; stored drives are rescored when the engine changes.
  • Data: local-first (up to 200 drives on device), mirrored to Supabase with owner-only row security. Sign-in via Apple or email. Server endpoints are Cloudflare Pages Functions, including the password-gated admin dashboard.
  • Deploy: push to main deploys web on Cloudflare and builds, signs and uploads iOS to TestFlight.
  • Process: two AI sessions push to main with a rebase loop, no force-push. Adversarial review on every change. package.json changes update both lockfiles. Rules in CLAUDE.md.

Open items

  • Scores are computed on the phone and uploaded; server-side scoring is needed before the leaderboard matters.
  • Database lockdown for three ID-trusting functions is built; it goes live when the migration is applied.
  • The auto-migrate job's secret is broken; migrations are applied by hand.
  • OBD trouble codes and VIN reading are untested on a real car.
  • No Android app.
  • Full tech-debt list is on the roadmap.

CMO

Marketing

Positioning

  • Smooth driving as a skill worth getting better at. Confident, slightly irreverent voice.
  • Use: smooth, efficient, reading the road, getting better, your data is yours.
  • Avoid: "safer driving," insurance, discounts, crash prevention.
  • Don't promise: redeemable points or an Android app (neither exists).
  • Competition: insurer tracking apps score drivers for the insurer; Waze rewards reporting, not driving.

Current state

  • Invite-only TestFlight; web app installs from smoothafdriving.pages.dev/install.
  • Built-in share points: post-drive score card, leaderboard, badges, Dynamic Island.
  • Candidate first audiences: driving enthusiasts, daily commuters, teens with parents.
  • smoothafdriving.com/tracker still serves an old tracker and should become a download link.

Known gaps

Known gaps

Calibration
Scoring thresholds are fitted to a few drivers' drives and will shift with more. Ride-composure thresholds are provisional.
Untested
Car Health and VIN reading are tested against reader reply formats, not a real car.
Security
Three database functions trusted a bare device or account ID. The fix is built and goes live with one database update: developer read functions are removed, and deleting, claiming or restoring a drive requires proof of ownership. Drives uploaded before Oct 17, 2026 stay reachable by device ID until claimed.
Integrity
Scores are computed on the phone, so a determined user could upload a score they didn't earn.
Braking
The approach-length half of the braking score doesn't vary between stops; only deceleration counts.
Portability
On a new phone, points for older drives are estimated because the cloud doesn't store them.
Rewards
Points can't be redeemed yet.
